--- title: About description: What Moving Maths is, how the pages are made, and how to contribute. ---

Moving Maths collects interactive pictures of mathematical ideas.

How the pages are made

Every page is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, made with the help of AI coding tools. Mathematics is written in TeX and typeset with KaTeX when the site is built.

Errors and contributions

If something is wrong, each page has a “Report an error” link at its foot that opens an issue on GitHub. The source of the site, with a guide for adding new pages, is in the repository.
