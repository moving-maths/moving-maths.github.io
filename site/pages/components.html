The theme's tokens and components, each in its states. Change a token or a component's rule in site/assets/css/theme.css and check it here; every page uses the same ones. See docs/design.md.

Colours

Blue is the primary accent (selected, links, focus); yellow the secondary (handles you can move). Data colours belong to the mathematics in figures, used in order; the first is the blue accent. Each has a pale -soft for fills, a -line for thick lines in several colours, and a darker -text for text and thin lines. Red is the mark (--highlight) and yellow the handle, outside the sequence; for a line or text in yellow use --accent-2-strong.
